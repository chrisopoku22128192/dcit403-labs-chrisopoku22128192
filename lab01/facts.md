# Department of Computer Science: Facts

1. The Department Office is in Room 104, Block C. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm.
2. The Head of Department is Dr. Ama Mensah. Her office is Room 110, Block C. Students see her by appointment, booked through the Department Office.

6. The IT Help Desk is in Block D, Room 201. It handles student email, Wi-Fi and portal password resets. It is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
7. The departmental library corner is in Block C, Room 115. Students can borrow up to 3 books for 2 weeks.
8. The Level 100 class rep coordinator is Ms. Efua Asante. She can be reached through the Department Office.
